## What's in this table

The curated form of the Home Credit `application_train` file (or the
synthetic set generated by `scripts/finance_private_synth.py`, same
columns): one row per application, with the outcome. It lives in the
project's own `finance_demo` dataset, which has no public IAM binding —
the crawler catalogued it through the same job that catalogues the public
tables, and discovery only offers it to users holding the
`finance.internal` entitlement.

`default_flag` is the Home Credit TARGET: 1 = the client had late payments
on at least one instalment. Base rate about 8%. There are no dates or
geographies in this data (the source is anonymised), so questions about
"this year" or "by state" cannot be answered from it; say so rather than
invent a period.

## Notes for query planning

- Prefer the templates: `ac.hc_default_rate_by_segment` for any
  "default rate by X" question; `ac.hc_bureau_history_vs_default` for
  anything about bureau inquiries or prior credits.
- `annual_income` and `credit_amount` are in the source's currency units
  (unstated); compare, don't convert.
- Small segments are noisy — the templates hide segments under a minimum
  applicant count and say so.
